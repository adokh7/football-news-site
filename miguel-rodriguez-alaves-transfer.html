Alavés Circle with €5m Offer as FC Utrecht Winger Miguel Rodríguez Faces Rapid Return to Spain

Transfer Insight 25 July 2026 4 min read
Share

Twelve months in football can feel like a lifetime, yet Miguel Rodríguez might not even get that long to unpack his bags in Utrecht. Barely a year after putting pen to paper on a permanent deal in the Eredivisie, the 23-year-old winger is already being drawn back to Spanish shores.

At a Glance
  • • Deportivo Alavés have submitted a formal €5m bid for winger Miguel Rodríguez
  • • FC Utrecht bought Rodríguez from Celta Vigo for just over €1m after a successful loan
  • • Celta Vigo retain a 50% sell-on clause, entitling them to half of any transfer fee agreed
  • • Rodríguez recorded 5 goals in 39 games at Utrecht after injury disruptions
  • • Prior season in Spain yielded 14 direct goal contributions in 25 games despite a groin injury
  • • Utrecht management will not block the transfer if valuation is met

A Whirlwind Twelve Months in the Netherlands

Deportivo Alavés have lodged a concrete €5 million offer for the former Celta Vigo starlet. Make no mistake: this is no sudden impulse buy from the Babazorros. Alavés have been lurking in the background for months, eyeing up the wide man to bolster an attack desperate to push well past last season's mid-table 14th-place finish.

Rodríguez arrived in Utrecht from Celta Vigo for a fee just over €1 million following a loan spell that convinced the Dutch outfit to lock him down. Now, barely a year into his Netherlands journey, Spain is calling him back.

The Numbers Game: Injury Frustration vs Lethal Potential

Honesty is required when auditing Rodríguez's maiden campaign at FC Utrecht. It was a fragmented, frustrating affair. Five goals in 39 outings across all competitions hardly reads like a world-beating return. But context matters. Persistent fitness breakdowns repeatedly crippled his rhythm just as he started gathering steam.

Rewind twelve months further, however, and you see precisely why Alavés refuse to let this go. During his final spell in Spain, Rodríguez posted 14 direct goal contributions across just 25 matches in league and cup play—even while battling a nagging groin problem. That is the explosive, vertical winger Alavés believe they are buying.

The 50% Celta Vigo Clause Shaping the Deal

Here is the financial sting tucked into the fine print. When Utrecht snapped up Rodríguez for a bargain fee just north of €1 million following an initial loan, Celta Vigo quietly inserted a massive 50 per cent sell-on clause into the contract.

That single detail alters the entire negotiation table. Half of whatever cheque Utrecht accept moves straight into Celta's bank account. Far from a minor footnote, it proves how shrewd clauses can pull the strings of a transfer long after a player walks out the exit door.

Utrecht's Stance and La Liga Redemption

For Utrecht, the stance is pragmatic. The Dutch hierarchy will not lock the doors or stand in the player's path. If Rodríguez wants another crack at Spain's top flight and Alavés match their valuation, Utrecht will shake hands and let him go.

With 26 La Liga appearances already under his belt from his time in Galicia, Rodríguez is hardly walking into uncharted waters. A true top-tier breakthrough eluded him at Celta, but Alavés offer the clean slate he needs. While bids can stall and paperwork can tear up at the final hour, a paper trail this clear suggests he will be wearing blue and white before long.
